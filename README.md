# MawarithLens
https://mawarithlens.onrender.com/

**MawarithLens** is an online Islamic inheritance (*Fara'id*) assistant that turns everyday family and estate stories into clear share breakdowns, primary references, and scholarly or legal review alerts.

---

## What It Does

- **Plain-Language Intake:** Describe the deceased, surviving family members, assets, debts, and any *wasiyyah* in natural language—or start with a ready-made sample scenario.
- **School of Jurisprudence Selection:** Switch between **Maliki** and **Hanafi** perspectives to see how rules and notes apply.
- **Share & Amount Breakdown:** View each heir's share, count, and estimated value alongside a visual share chart.
- **Qur'an, Hadith & Scholarly References:** Inspect primary textual references and scholarly notes for each heir share.
- **Scholar & Legal Review Alerts:** Automatically flags complex family situations, missing details, or statutory, will, and customary land overlaps for qualified human review.
- **English & Arabic Toggle:** Switch the interface and generated results between **English** and **العربية (Arabic)** with right-to-left layout support.

---

## How to Use It

1. **Open MawarithLens** in your browser. https://mawarithlens.onrender.com/
2. **Choose your preferences** in the sidebar: select your preferred school (**Maliki** or **Hanafi**), pick a sample scenario if you want a quick example, and choose **English** or **العربية (Arabic)**.
3. **Enter the case details** in the story box—including surviving relatives, properties or savings, debts, funeral costs, and any *wasiyyah*.
4. **Click "Analyze Estate Distribution"** to view the summary, heir shares, references, share chart, and any review notice.

---

## Important Notice

MawarithLens is provided for educational and informational purposes only and is **not** a formal *Fatwa* or a court ruling. Always consult a qualified Sharia scholar—and a legal or estate practitioner where marriage laws, wills, or property titles are involved—before distributing an estate.
